```
    /testimonials/

```

### Naming Convention
```
[category]-[description]-[variant].[ext]
Examples:
portrait-arnaud-cheynut-primary-raw.cr2
portrait-arnaud-cheynut-landscape-web.jpg
office-reception-wide-raw.dng
logo-primary.svg
logo-white.svg
bio-francais.txt
expertise-penal-description.txt
mentions-legales.md
```

### Image Optimization (Dev Team Handles)
- Source: Keep originals
- Web: WebP (primary), AVIF (modern), JPG fallback
- Sizes: 320w, 640w, 1200w, 1920w (responsive)
- Quality: 85% (photos), 90% (UI)
- Blur placeholders: 20px wide base64
